PROJECT RECAP: "BELOW THE DEAD" (zombie game, owner: Andrea) — updated at btd-v36 (full recap for a new chat)

WHAT IT IS
- Mobile, landscape-only, top-down twin-stick zombie shooter. HTML5 canvas, single index.html plus sprites/, offline PWA. No PvP.
- 5 heroes: Maya (crossbow), Ben (bat + shield), Dr. Aris (flare gun, medic), Chloe (knives), Leo (punk, guitar, nailgun, unlockable; Maya's little brother).
- 4 chapters (Streets, Metro, Lab, Crypt) x 10 stages (1-1 ... 1-10) + 4 "coming soon" teaser chapters, bosses, safe-house holds, Hideout base, shop, lab, kitchen, missions.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages: aderoasted.github.io/below_the_dead. Goal: beta on Google Play, itch.io, CrazyGames, Poki.
- User is careful about copyright. Always double-check.

FILES
- index.html: all code. Sprite metadata inline (SPR). New entries are added after the line "SPR.bloater.s.die = ...".
- sprites/*.webp atlases. sw.js cache version: btd-v36 (bump every release).
- Sprite pipeline: Gemini sheet on magenta -> key (with magenta unmixing) -> slice per frame -> pack WebP atlas -> SPR JSON.
- ALL BALANCING NUMBERS: the "TUNE" block right after "const VIEW_H = 450" (search "TUNE · ALL BALANCING").

DONE IN v30 (Batch 2 + Batch 3)
Progression
- Old blood upgrades (Save.up) removed. One-time migration refunds all blood spent on them (banner "UPGRADES REWORKED").
- Per-hero levels 1-30 earned only by play (Save.hxp): kills, elites, bosses, stage/chapter clears, daily missions, training, first win. Each level: +2.5% HP, +2.5% dmg, +0.5% speed, +2% special charge.
- Weapon levels 0-10 for every weapon incl. signature ones (+6% dmg each, 1 XP per kill). Old weapon XP carries over on the new curve.
- Special levels 0-10 (Save.spx, 1 XP per use, +6% special power).
- Ultimate is now a recharge timer: 45/60/75 s (Easy/Medium/Hard), kills shave 0.35 s, starts 25% charged. Gear item 4 = 12% faster (was "-6 kills"). Perk "ultfast" = 15% faster.
- Aris's special also heals allies nearby (companion, tutorial actors) and revives a downed companion.
- Survivors screen shows hero level/XP, weapon level and special level. Results show hero XP, weapon XP, dark blood.
Balancing
- Difficulties differ in: zombie HP/damage, spawn speed, elite chance, zombie speed, medkit drops, dark-blood chance, gate quotas, ultimate time, free frost bombs.
- Kill-quota gates: 2 gates per stage at 34% and 68% (3 parts). Quota grows per chapter and difficulty. Replaces the single roadblock.
- Walls: base 90 HP, 12 levels of +15% (old levels doubled). Med Bay: 10 smaller levels (old doubled). Safe-house kit walls use the new wall HP.
- Lab: free, unlocks after clearing stage 1-3. Six research lines with small tiers paid in blood: Steel (10x +6%), Nano-Repair (8x +8%), Electric (8x +10%), MG Nest (6), Cryo (6, frost bombs), Field Serum (8x +4% medkits). Old research converted (steel = tier 5, others tier 1).
- Kitchen: no longer bought. Free by rescuing Chef Rocco in stage 1-5. Players who already built it keep it.
- Frost bombs: new ❄ button (key G). Slow zombies 6 s in a radius, chill bosses. Free per level: Easy 2, Medium 1, Hard 0. Shop pack 3 for 300 blood (max 9 stored). Elites can drop them.
- Dark blood 🖤 (Save.dark): from bosses (3/4/6) and elites (6/10/14% by difficulty), robot dog gives 3. Shown in HUD, shop, Hideout.
- Cat helpers: first cat free. Cats bring 30 blood/hour each while away (cap 8 h, collected when opening the Hideout). In levels a cat follows you and grabs blood you leave behind. More cats: 15/30/50 dark blood or IAP placeholder €0.99 (max 4).
- One-page Armory: 9 cards (5 signature + 4 extra weapons) with icon, level, description, how to get, buy/equip. No scrolling.
- Daily hooks: first win of the day (+150 blood, +100 hero XP), daily Training cap (400 weapon XP/day), cats while away, daily missions now also give hero XP. Nothing sells levels.
Tutorial and story
- New story engine: dialogue popups (tap to continue, emoji portraits, italic narration), "TRY IT" hints with a ring on the button.
- Stage 1-1 tutorial (Save.tut2): Maya MOVE+AIM, Chloe ROLL, Aris SPECIAL (vent bite, flare heals everyone), Leo SWAP, Ben ULTIMATE (horde, EARTHQUAKE), then call partner + auto-aim, Aris "let's go up". Few zombies, each used for a button. SKIP TUTORIAL button. Special used in the tutorial doesn't burn Hard's one special; auto-aim is free during it.
- Easy: partner arrives when you call. Medium/Hard: partner helps until gate 1 (first third), leaves for 20 s, then you call them back.
- Leo in the tutorial: guest NPC (not unlocked). He shows up, does his guitar demo, lends you a machete if you have no 2nd weapon, then runs to the Metro ("Gotta run, sis! The Metro needs me!" / Maya: "LEO! ...He never listens."). This sets up the RESCUE LEO mission.
- Stage 1-5 Chef Rocco quest (Save.rocco): Meeting (Leo laughs "on the radio" if not unlocked yet), First sighting (robot dog flees), Wrong arm (mannequin arm after gate 1), robot dog fight after gate 2 (boss arena stays shut until it's beaten), Reunion -> free Kitchen + "Rocco cooks" in the kitchen.

DONE IN v31 (gamepad)
- Any USB/Bluetooth gamepad or cheap joystick works (browser Gamepad API). Detected on first button press; banner "CONTROLLER READY".
- In levels: left stick or D-pad = move · right stick = aim + fire · A roll · B frost bomb · X special · Y swap · LB call partner · RB ultimate · RT fire (hold, for pads without a right stick) · R3 auto-aim · START pause. Rumble on pads that support it.
- Menus: a yellow cursor moves with the left stick/D-pad, A clicks whatever is under it (works on the Hideout canvas too), B goes back/closes, right stick scrolls lists. A advances story popups and comics.
- Settings → Controls → 🎮 GAMEPAD: shows the connected pad, lets players reassign every button (buttons or trigger axes) and both sticks (push right, then down), dead zone LOW/MID/HIGH, RESET BUTTONS. Saved in Cfg.pad.
- Touch sticks hide while a pad is in use; touching the screen brings them back. Tutorial hints show pad button names when a pad is active.
- Fix: the tutorial hint box now only shows while a hint is active.
- Tested with a simulated non-standard pad (remap, move, aim/fire, menus, Hideout rooms, back). Not yet tested on a real controller.
